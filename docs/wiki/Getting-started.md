# Getting started

You need internet access for your first sign-in and the email and password of the pre-authorized owner account. Access is provisioned by the app operator; there is no signup button.

1. Open [DC Training Log](https://dc-training.vercel.app) in your browser.
2. Enter your email and password and select **SIGN IN**. Wait for Home and **SYNCED**.
3. Open **ROTATION**. Select the next workout shown on Home and assign every required body part. Choose an exercise, its available protocol, and a target range or structure. Review the assignment and select **SAVE**.
4. Return to **HOME**. Missing assignments are listed next to the workout start control. Once complete, select **START A1** (or the slot Home shows).
5. Read the displayed prescription and previous performance. Choose the load type, enter the performed reps or duration, and select **SAVE & NEXT**. For rest-pause, enter **RP1**, **RP2**, and **RP3**; the total is calculated for you.
6. Work through the exercises and stretches, explicitly skipping any step you do not perform. Resolve any logbook decision shown. When the workout is complete, select **DONE**.
7. Home shows the next slot. Open **HISTORY → WORKOUTS** to inspect the saved workout. Confirm **SYNCED** before relying on another device.

If Home shows **CRUISE / RECOVERY**, training is paused. Select **START NEW BLAST** only when you intend to resume training; the preserved next slot continues.

## Keep the app handy

The app includes a web-app manifest and offline shell. Use your browser's install or Add to Home Screen option when offered. Installation is optional; the browser app works at the same URL. Sign in online before relying on offline access. Browser installation controls vary; the repository does not provide a native App Store package.

## If you cannot get in

- On the sign-in page, enter your email before selecting **FORGOT PASSWORD?** Open the recovery email and complete the password form.
- A configuration or account-check error requires operator help if retrying online does not resolve it.
- A pending deletion opens the recovery screen instead of Home. Follow [account recovery](https://github.com/mbh-solutions/dc_training/wiki/Offline-and-account).
